# Mission Reflection

Completing the Multi-Cloud Explorer laboratory helped me understand AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud better. Before doing this activity, I mostly thought of cloud platforms as places where websites and applications are hosted. As I researched them, I learned that they offer many different services for computing, storage, networking, databases, security, artificial intelligence, and application development. Among the three, AWS impressed me the most because it has a wide variety of services and a large global infrastructure. I also found Azure useful because of its strong connection with Microsoft technologies, while Google Cloud stood out to me because of its focus on artificial intelligence, machine learning, data, and Kubernetes.

I noticed that AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud are also very similar in many ways. All three offer virtual machines, storage, networking, databases, identity management, and container services. They also have tools for managing and monitoring cloud resources. Even though the services have different names, they usually perform similar functions.

I learned that business requirements are very important when choosing a cloud provider. A company should not simply choose the most popular platform. It should consider its budget, existing technologies, type of workload, security needs, number of users, scalability, and other requirements. For example, Azure would be a good choice for a company already using Microsoft technologies, while Google Cloud could be a better option for a company focused on AI and machine learning.

This activity also helped me understand what it means to think like a Cloud Solutions Consultant. I learned that there is no single cloud provider that is best for everyone. The right choice depends on what the client actually needs.

Lastly, my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved because I added research, comparisons, recommendations, screenshots, Linux investigation results, and my reflection. Overall, this activity gave me a better understanding of how cloud platforms are used in real-world situations and how to choose one based on a client's needs.
